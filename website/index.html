What it does

From MakerWorld to your print bed in a minute

Download a project, drop it on PlateShift, and open the result in your own slicer.

Keeps the colours

Painted colours move to your toolheads or CFS slots. Too many colours? Similar ones are merged for you, and you can change any of it.

The right filament

Matches each colour to a real filament preset, not just a material type, so Silk PLA stays Silk PLA. Or pick your own presets.

Your print profile

Keeps the designer's walls, infill and supports, while speeds and accelerations come from your printer's own profile. Or use your own profile.

Always-current profiles

No built-in copies to go out of date: PlateShift uses the profiles your slicer installed, including your own tweaks, so slicer updates reach it too.

Fits your bed

Objects and prime towers are moved to suit a different bed size, with a warning if anything won't fit.

Catches problems early

Warns before you slice, for example when two filaments need different nozzle temperatures on a one-nozzle printer.

Test slice and cost

Optionally slices the result in the background to prove it works, and reports the print time, filament weight and what the print costs.

Send to your printer

Sends the print to your Flashforge or Creality printer over your own network, with each filament on the right slot and a preview picture on its screen, and starts it if you ask.

Batch & command line

Convert a whole folder at once, open 3MFs straight from your Downloads, or script it from the command line.

Private by design

Nothing ever goes online

PlateShift is a small program that runs entirely on your own PC or Mac. It reads the printer profiles your slicer already installed and rewrites the project file locally. It never connects to the internet, never asks you to sign in and never sends your files anywhere, except to your own printer when you ask it to.

  • Works fully offline: no internet needed, and it only talks to your printer when you send a print
  • No account, no analytics, no telemetry: not even an update check
  • Never changes behind your back: a new version arrives only when you download one
  • No ads, no affiliate links, no upsell
  • Your original file is never changed
  • Your printer's IP address and access keys are never copied into projects
  • Open source (AGPL): read every line, build it yourself, improve it

Questions

Frequently asked

The short answers. The README on GitHub has the details.

Can I print MakerWorld models on a Flashforge or Creality printer?

Yes. Download the 3MF project from MakerWorld, drop it on PlateShift, pick your printer and convert. The copy opens in Flash Studio, OrcaSlicer or Creality Print set up for your printer, with its plates, objects and painted colours.

Does it keep the colours of multicolour models?

Yes. Painted colours move to your printer's toolheads (Creator 5) or slots (AD5X, Creality CFS). If a project has more colours than your printer, similar ones are merged for you, and you can change any of it.

Which printers does PlateShift support?

Flashforge Creator 5 Pro, Creator 5, AD5X, Adventurer 5M and 5M Pro, and the Creality K1, K1C, K1 SE, K1 Max (with or without the CFS), K2, K2 Plus, K2 Pro, K2 SE and SparkX i7 and i8.

Do I need anything else installed?

A slicer that has your printer's profiles: Flash Studio or OrcaSlicer for Flashforge, OrcaSlicer or Creality Print for Creality. PlateShift reads the profiles your slicer already installed.

Can it send the print straight to my printer?

Yes, for Flashforge (Creator 5, AD5X, Adventurer 5M) and Creality printers on stock firmware (K1 family, K2, SparkX). After converting, Send to printer… uploads the print over your own network, with each filament on the matching slot, and starts it only if you tick Start printing now. Flashforge printers also need their serial number and check code, which PlateShift reads from Flash Studio if it has connected to the printer.

Is it free? Does it upload my files?

It's free and open source (GNU AGPL), with no ads. It never connects to the internet: no uploads, no analytics, no automatic updates. Everything happens on your own computer, and your original file is never changed.

Does it work on Mac and Linux?

Yes. There's a Windows program, a Mac app and a Linux AppImage to download. On Linux it can also run from source with Python.
