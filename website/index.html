What it does

From MakerWorld to your print bed in a minute

Download a project, drop it on PlateShift, and open the result in your own slicer.

Keeps the colours

Painted colours move to your toolheads or CFS slots. Too many colours? Similar ones are merged for you, and you can change any of it.

Your filaments

Picks the closest filament preset for every colour, or use your own presets from Flash Studio, OrcaSlicer or Creality Print.

Your print profile

Automatic, or your own print profile. Keep the designer's walls, infill and supports, or use yours.

Fits your bed

Objects and prime towers are moved to suit a different bed size, with a warning if anything won't fit.

Test slice

Optionally slices the result in the background to prove it works, and reports the print time and filament weight.

Batch & command line

Convert a whole folder at once, or script it from the command line.

Private by design

Nothing ever leaves your computer

PlateShift is a small program that runs entirely on your own PC or Mac. It reads the printer profiles your slicer already installed and rewrites the project file locally. It never connects to the internet, never asks you to sign in and never sends your files anywhere.

  • Works fully offline: unplug the network and it still works
  • No account, no tracking, no telemetry
  • Your original file is never changed
  • Your printer's IP address and access keys are never copied into projects
  • Open source, so anyone can check exactly what it does

How it works

Three steps

Pick your printer

Choose your printer, and if you like, your own print profile.

Drop in the 3MF

Check which colour goes on which toolhead or slot, then click Convert.

Open and print

Open the converted copy in your slicer with File → Open Project.

Linux instructions

The apps aren't code-signed yet. The first time, Windows may say it protected your PC: choose More info → Run anyway. On a Mac, open it once, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway (on macOS 14 and earlier, right-click PlateShift and choose Open instead).
